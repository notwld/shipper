Money-Back Guarantee / Refund Policy

Any funds deposited for shipping services are non-refundable once the shipment has been dispatched. Refunds will only be issued if the shipment is canceled before dispatch, and a service fee may apply. However, if *BeMyShipper* cancels or terminates the service for reasons other than your violation or non-performance, a refund may be provided.

Refund Policy / Money Back Guarantee

- Any funds deposited for a shipment shall not be refundable once the shipment is processed or dispatched.
- If a cancellation request is made before the shipment has been dispatched, you may be eligible for a full refund (minus any service or processing fees).
- If a refund request is made within 48 hours after shipment processing, you may be eligible for a 50% refund (minus any service or processing fees).
- Refunds will not be provided once a shipment has been delivered or after it has left the facility.
