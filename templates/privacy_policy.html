Privacy Policy

About The Policy

At BeMyShipper, we prioritize the privacy and security of our customers. We collect data solely to enhance our services and ensure an optimal shipping experience. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information, and how you can manage your privacy preferences.

Collected Personal Information

BeMyShipper collects personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address. These details are provided when placing an order or signing up for services. We may also collect information about your preferences, previous orders, and interactions with our platform to improve our offerings.

Utilization of Collected Data

The data we collect is used to process orders, communicate with you about your shipments, and enhance our platform's user experience. We may send emails regarding order updates, promotional offers, and newsletters tailored to your preferences and previous interactions.

Newsletter Subscription

If you no longer wish to receive our newsletters or promotional emails, you can unsubscribe by following the instructions in the email or by contacting us at customersupport@bemyshipper.com or calling 1 (703) 975-6454.

Widgets and Social Media Features

Our website includes social media features such as the Facebook Like button, which may collect information like your IP address and the page you are viewing. These features may also use cookies to function properly.

Third-Party Intervention

We do not share your personal information with third parties except where necessary for business operations, such as credit card processing or live customer support. These third parties are authorized to use your information solely for the services they provide to us.

Personal Information Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption, to protect your personal information during transmission. While we strive to ensure the security of your data, no method of transmission over the internet is entirely secure. For any security concerns, contact us at customersupport@bemyshipper.com.

Registered Account Access

Customers can log into their accounts to view and update their personal information and previous orders. To delete or modify your account, contact us via email. We will retain your information as required by law or to fulfill our contractual obligations.

Cookies and Their Applications

We use cookies to personalize your experience and track site usage. Cookies help store your preferences and shopping cart items during visits. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings, but disabling cookies may impact your ability to use certain features of our website.

Web Beacons/Web Bugs (Clear Gifs)

We use clear gifs (web beacons) to track user activity on our site and improve content delivery. These do not collect personally identifiable information, but are used to analyze site performance and user behavior.

Customer Feedback

We may post your testimonial, with your permission, on our site. If you wish to have your testimonial removed, please email us at customersupport@bemyshipper.com.

Other Websites Links

BeMyShipper's website may contain links to third-party sites. These sites have their own privacy policies, and we recommend reviewing them before submitting any personal information.

Notification of Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be posted on this page and communicated through email or other means, ensuring you are aware of how we handle your information.

Disclaimer of Liability

BeMyShipper is not responsible for the disclosure of your personal information due to legal requirements or court orders. We will only share your personal information when required by law or to protect our rights.

Questions

If you have any questions regarding our Terms and Conditions or how we use your personal data, please contact us at customersupport@bemyshipper.com or call 1 (703) 975-6454
